PROJECT NAME: AssetTrackr

========================================
FRONTEND ARCHITECTURE (UI TREE)
========================================

----------------------------------------
Role: IT Administrator (Full access to manage assets and employees)
----------------------------------------

Page: /dashboard (Main landing page after login)

Component: <Sidebar /> - Navigation links (Dashboard, Assets, Employees, Assignments)

Component: <TopNavbar /> - Displays admin name, profile, logout button

Component: <StatCards /> - Shows Total Assets, Assigned Assets, Available Assets

Component: <RecentAssignmentsTable /> - Shows latest asset assignments


Page: /assets (Manage all assets)

Component: <Sidebar /> - Reused navigation

Component: <TopNavbar /> - Reused header

Component: <AssetsTable /> - Table showing all assets with search, filter, pagination

Component: <AddAssetModal /> - Form popup to add new asset

Component: <EditAssetModal /> - Form popup to update asset details

Component: <DeleteAssetButton /> - Button to remove asset

Component: <FilterBar /> - Filter by status (Available, Assigned, Maintenance)


Page: /employees (Manage employees)

Component: <Sidebar /> - Reused

Component: <TopNavbar /> - Reused

Component: <EmployeesTable /> - Table of all employees

Component: <AddEmployeeModal /> - Form to add employee

Component: <EditEmployeeModal /> - Update employee info

Component: <DeleteEmployeeButton /> - Remove employee


Page: /assignments (Track asset assignments)

Component: <Sidebar /> - Reused

Component: <TopNavbar /> - Reused

Component: <AssignmentsTable /> - Shows all asset assignments

Component: <AssignAssetModal /> - Assign asset to employee

Component: <ReturnAssetButton /> - Mark asset as returned

Component: <FilterBar /> - Filter by Active / Returned


Page: /reports (Analytics & overview)

Component: <Sidebar /> - Reused

Component: <TopNavbar /> - Reused

Component: <Charts /> - Graphs showing asset usage statistics

Component: <SummaryCards /> - Key insights (total usage, returns, etc.)


----------------------------------------
Role: Standard Employee (Limited access)
----------------------------------------

Page: /my-assets (Employee dashboard)

Component: <TopNavbar /> - Shows employee name and logout

Component: <MyAssetsList /> - Cards showing assigned assets

Component: <AssetCard /> - Individual asset info (name, status, serial number)

Component: <ReportIssueButton /> - Button to report damaged asset


Page: /history (View past assets)

Component: <TopNavbar /> - Reused

Component: <HistoryTable /> - List of previously assigned assets

Component: <FilterBar /> - Filter by returned/active


----------------------------------------
Role: HR/Admin (Optional role - user management focus)
----------------------------------------

Page: /hr-dashboard

Component: <Sidebar /> - HR-specific navigation

Component: <TopNavbar /> - Reused

Component: <EmployeeStats /> - Total employees, departments

Component: <RecentJoins /> - Recently added employees


Page: /hr/employees

Component: <Sidebar /> - Reused

Component: <TopNavbar /> - Reused

Component: <EmployeesTable /> - View all employees

Component: <AddEmployeeModal /> - Add new employee

Component: <SearchBar /> - Search employees
